# Process Guide walkthrough design

Concept: a patient instructor points to real controls while the app remains readable.

Palette: navy #0c2432; white #f4f8fa; muted #c4d1d6; teal #63c9ba; highlight #24b8a0; border #344c59; deep #05131b and #071d29; panel #eaf1f3; secondary #89a3af and #b0c3cc.

Typography: locally embedded Inter, named TourInter, 400 body and 700 title. Single-family choice deliberately follows the actual product UI. Title55px, body26px, caption33px, labels18–20px.

Layout: 60px edge anchors; narrative rail385px; actual screenshot frame1350×730;14–20px corners; restrained drop shadow.

Motion: seek-safe slow1.015× camera move, exact-target clockwise outline draw, steady progress bar. Screens cut at authored boundaries. No generated UI, stock imagery, ornamental effects, or music masking speech.
