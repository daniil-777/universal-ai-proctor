- This is real manufacturing footage in a company promotional film. Shots show several machines, tools, moulds, and products; they do not establish one continuous production job or the history of one tracked workpiece.
- Use the steps as viewing checkpoints in the film's order. Mark visible activities observed; do not treat all checkpoints as proof that a part is completed, inspected, accepted, or ready for use.
- Approximate time windows refer to the playback. Crossfades and short inserts can obscure tool contact or chronology; record uncertainty rather than inferring an operation.
- Names such as mould, controller, and cutting tool describe visible context. Do not infer machine programs, cutting parameters, tool identity, material grade, tolerances, calibration, or metrology results from appearance alone.
- A controller display, tool rack, CAD drawing, polished surface, or finished-looking part is not evidence of a passing quality-control result.
- The guide is an educational observation aid. Production instructions, machine access, safe setup, guarding, energy isolation, and acceptance criteria come from the responsible workplace and manufacturer.

Step 1: Observe machining context and controller interaction
Objective: Around 00:00-00:30, identify the tooling context and an operator at a machine controller.
Tools: Visible tooling or workpiece; machining enclosure; controller console.
Actions:
- Observe the early close-up tooling views and the machine work area.
- Observe the operator interacting with the controller console.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: A machine work area with a mounted tool or workpiece is visible.
- Visual observation: An operator is visibly present at a controller console and interacts with its controls.

Step 2: Observe machine-tool activity
Objective: Around 00:30-00:52, recognize a visible tool-and-workpiece operation in the machine views.
Tools: Machine spindle or cutting tool; mounted workpiece; visible fixture.
Actions:
- Observe the relative movement of the tool and the mounted workpiece.
- Identify only the contact region visible in the current shot; keep obscured contact uncertain.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: The mounted workpiece and tool region appear together in the machine view.
- Visual observation: Visible motion occurs in the tool or workpiece region.
- Visual observation: A later close-up shows a machining or tool region from a different viewpoint.

Step 3: Observe tool presentation and manual tooling work
Objective: Around 00:52-01:15, recognize the displayed tools, digital indicator, and hands-on mould or tooling activity.
Tools: Displayed tool rack and control unit; digital indicator with a probe; tooling assembly; handheld implement where visible.
Actions:
- Observe the row of tools and the adjacent display/control unit.
- Observe the digital indicator and its probe shown above a metal block.
- Observe hands working at the exposed tooling assembly.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: Several tools are presented in a rack with a display or control unit nearby.
- Visual observation: A digital indicator with a probe is shown above a metal block.
- Visual observation: Hands and an exposed tooling assembly appear together during manual work.

Step 4: Observe product presentation and further tooling operations
Objective: Around 01:15-01:55, distinguish the presented product from subsequent machine-tool footage.
Tools: Displayed manufactured component; metal blocks or tooling; machine-tool work area.
Actions:
- Observe the isolated product shown against the light background.
- Observe the subsequent close-up tool-and-metal-block views and later tooling-machine shots.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: A manufactured component is visibly presented separately from the machine footage.
- Visual observation: A later machine view shows a tool positioned over mounted metal blocks or tooling.

Step 5: Observe sample parts and collaborative drawing review
Objective: Around 01:55-02:20, identify the sample-part display and the group reviewing a drawing.
Tools: Displayed coloured sample parts; drawing or plan board.
Actions:
- Observe the group of coloured sample parts.
- Observe people pointing to or discussing a drawing displayed on a board.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: Multiple coloured sample parts are shown together.
- Visual observation: A group stands beside a drawing or plan, with a visible pointing gesture toward it.

Step 6: Observe final machine and mould-detail views
Objective: Around 02:20-02:55, identify the finishing montage's machine motion and exposed mould detail.
Tools: Visible machine component; open mould or tooling assembly.
Actions:
- Observe the moving machine component in the close-up shot.
- Observe the subsequent exposed mould or tooling detail and the film's closing views.
Criteria:
- Visual observation: A machine component visibly moves or rotates in close-up.
- Visual observation: An exposed mould or tooling assembly is shown with its cavities or internal features visible.
