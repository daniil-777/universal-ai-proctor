This real gym video mixes explanations, edited demonstrations and promotional ending cards. The guide covers visible equipment and movement, not the speaker's unverified claims about benefits, comfort or suitability. Approximate timestamps refer to real-05-sport.mp4.

Step 1: Identify the EZ bar and visible starting grip
Objective: Approximate anchor 00:35-00:38. Observe the first demonstrator preparing the bent-shaft bar.
Tools: EZ bar with visible plates; gym floor.
Actions:
- Locate the angled bar as the performer reaches down toward it.
- Observe both hands contacting the bar and the performer rising to an upright position.
- Identify the visible underhand grip and the bar held low in front of the thighs in the close view.
Criteria:
- A bar with angled shaft sections and plates is visible.
- Both hands visibly hold the bar in the starting close view.
- The bar is visibly below the torso with the arms extended downward in that view.
Warnings: The footage does not establish plate weight or whether clips are fitted. Do not infer equipment checks from a completed pickup.

Step 2: Observe an EZ-bar raise and lowering in the close view
Objective: Approximate anchor 00:39-00:43. Review the close-view motion before the wider camera framing.
Tools: Visible EZ bar and plates.
Actions:
- Track the bar from its low position toward the upper torso while both hands hold it.
- Observe the increase in elbow bend in the raised position.
- Follow the bar back toward the low position within this view.
Criteria:
- The bar is visibly closer to the upper torso in a raised position.
- Both hands remain visibly on the bar in the raised position.
- A subsequent low bar position is visible with less elbow bend in the same view.
Warnings: The head and feet are cropped. Camera movement and blur prevent precise angle, tempo, load or whole-body posture measurement.

Criteria:

Warnings: Plate weight, equipment security and preparation checks are unverified. Do not infer those checks from the pickup, or compare this person's comfort with the earlier performer.
