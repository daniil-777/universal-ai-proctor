Description of a Scene
Arthroscopic intra-articular shoulder scene during Bankart repair, viewed mainly from a posterior portal. Visible structures include the humeral head, glenoid, anterior-inferior labrum, capsule, and glenoid neck. Instruments enter through anterior portals to assess instability, mobilize the capsulolabral tissue, prepare bone, insert suture anchors, pass sutures, tie or secure fixation, and confirm a stable restored labral bumper.

Step 1: Portals
Objective:
Establish posterior, mid-anterior, and anterosuperior portals to enable visualization and instrument access to the glenohumeral joint, including assessment of the posterior humeral head and anteroinferior glenoid/labrum.

Expected_instruments:

Arthroscope

Posterior cannula

Mid-anterior cannula

Anterosuperior cannula / arthroscopic sheath / switching stick

Spinal needles

Trocar

Actions:

Establish posterior portal

Insert posterior cannula and arthroscope

Obtain initial intra-articular view

Visualize posterior humeral head

Inspect posterior humeral head

Assess for Hill-Sachs lesion

Introduce mid-anterior spinal needle

Insert needle superior to subscapularis

Direct toward anteroinferior glenoid/labrum

Establish mid-anterior cannula

Advance trocar through capsule

Position cannula adjacent to subscapularis

Demonstrate instrument access

Introduce instrument via mid-anterior portal

Confirm reach to anteroinferior glenoid/labrum

Introduce anterosuperior spinal needle

Insert at rotator interval

Direct toward anterior glenoid

Establish anterosuperior cannula

Advance trocar through capsule

Position cannula intra-articularly

Completion_criteria:

Clear posterior intra-articular view obtained

Mid-anterior cannula placed in the joint

Anterosuperior cannula placed in the joint

Errors:

Posterior portal malposition

Inadequate visualization from posterior portal

Failure to visualize posterior humeral head

Missed Hill-Sachs lesion

Incorrect spinal needle trajectory

Needle not placed superior to subscapularis

Cannula not abutting subscapularis

Poor mid-anterior cannula positioning

Inadequate instrument access to anteroinferior glenoid/labrum

Incorrect anterosuperior needle entry point

Poor needle direction toward anterior glenoid

Improper anterosuperior cannula placement

Limited access through anterosuperior portal

Typical_duration:
2–4 minutes


Step 2: Arthroscopic Instability Assessment
Objective:
Systematically assess intra-articular structures for instability-related pathology, including labrum, rotator cuff, glenoid, and capsuloligamentous structures.

Expected_instruments:

Arthroscope

Probe

Arthroscopic sheath

Cannula

Actions:

Assess superior labrum

Visualize superior labral attachment onto glenoid

Direct probe toward superior labrum if needed

Assess articular surface of rotator cuff

Visualize articular side of superior cuff

Direct probe toward cuff if needed

Assess anteroinferior glenoid / Bankart pathology

Direct probe toward anteroinferior glenoid

Evaluate for Bankart lesion, rim fracture, or articular defect

Assess anterior-inferior glenohumeral ligaments (mid-substance)

Visualize mid-substance of ligaments

Direct probe toward capsular tissue if needed

Assess humeral insertion of anterior glenohumeral ligaments (HAGL)

Visualize ligament insertion on anterior humeral neck

Evaluate for HAGL lesion

Completion_criteria:

Probe or arthroscope directed to the anterior-inferior labrum / glenoid region

Diagnostic inspection of the instability lesion visibly performed

Errors:

Loss of intra-articular position of scope, sheath, or cannula during superior labral assessment

Loss of intra-articular position during rotator cuff assessment

Loss of intra-articular position during Bankart pathology assessment

Loss of intra-articular position during capsuloligamentous assessment

Loss of intra-articular position during HAGL assessment

Typical_duration:
2–3 minutes


Step 3: Capsulolabral Mobilization / Glenoid Preparation
Objective:
Mobilize the capsulolabral tissue, restore capsular tension, and prepare the anterior glenoid neck for repair.

Expected_instruments:

Elevator

Grasper

Mechanical abrasion instrument

Arthroscope

Cannula

Actions:

Mobilize capsulolabral tissue

Elevate capsulolabral tissue from the glenoid neck and articular margin

Maintain controlled tissue elevation during mobilization

Confirm adequate mobilization

Visualize subscapularis muscle superficial to the mobilized capsule

Confirm sufficient capsular release and excursion

Perform inferior-to-superior capsular shift

Grasp capsulolabral tissue

Shift tissue superiorly to restore tension

Visualize anterior glenoid neck

Obtain clear view of anterior glenoid neck

Maintain exposure for preparation

Prepare glenoid neck

Mechanically abrade anterior glenoid neck

Prepare bone surface in a controlled manner

Completion_criteria:

Capsulolabral tissue elevated from the glenoid neck

Anterior glenoid neck clearly exposed

Glenoid neck visibly prepared with the abrasion instrument

Errors:

Laceration of intact tissue (SENTINEL)

Loss of control during capsulolabral mobilization

Failure to mobilize capsulolabral tissue adequately

Poor inferior-to-superior shift

Inadequate capsular tension restoration

Inadequate exposure of anterior glenoid neck

Over-preparation of glenoid neck

Under-preparation of glenoid neck

Loss of control during glenoid preparation (SENTINEL)

Typical_duration:
2–4 minutes


Step 4: Inferior Anchor Preparation / Insertion
Objective:
Prepare, place, and test the most inferior suture anchor for capsulolabral repair.

Expected_instruments:

Drill guide

Drill

Suture anchor

Anchor inserter

Arthroscope

Cannula

Actions:

Position drill guide for inferior anchor

Seat the guide for the most inferior anchor hole

Confirm guide position at the intended inferior anchor site

Drill anchor hole

Drill obliquely to the glenoid articular face

Withdraw drill after completing tunnel

Insert suture anchor

Advance anchor into prepared tunnel

Withdraw inserter after anchor placement

Test anchor fixation

Apply visible tension to anchor sutures

Confirm anchor stability under tension

Completion_criteria:

Drill guide seated at the inferior anchor site

Drilling of the anchor hole clearly performed

Inferior anchor inserted and seated in bone

Errors:

Guide not positioned in inferior quadrant

Tunnel entry outside 0-3 mm safe zone (SENTINEL)

Cartilage damage during drilling

Implant remains proud (SENTINEL)

Implant breakage

Poor anchor depth

Anchor instability during testing

Typical_duration:
1–2 minutes

Step 5: Suture Delivery / Management (Inferior Anchor)
Objective:
Pass and shuttle the anchor suture limb through the capsular tissue for the inferior repair construct.

Expected_instruments:

Cannulated suture hook or retriever

Shuttle suture device

Suture limb

Arthroscope

Cannula

Actions:

Penetrate capsular tissue

Pass a cannulated suture hook or retriever through capsular tissue

Withdraw the device after tissue penetration

Shuttle suture limb

Pass anchor suture limb through capsular tissue

Deliver the suture limb out the cannula

Completion_criteria:

Suture-passing device penetrates the capsular tissue

Anchor suture limb passes through the tissue

Passed suture limb is delivered out the cannula

Errors:

Instrument breakage

Tissue damage during capsular penetration

Capsular penetration at or superior to anchor (SENTINEL)

Penetration not at capsulolabral junction

Tissue tearing

Suture or device entanglement

Off-loading anchor

Typical_duration:
1–2 minutes

Step 6: Knot-Tying / Knotless Construct (Inferior Anchor)
Objective:
Secure the inferior repair construct and finalize fixation.

Expected_instruments:

Knot pusher or knotless fixation device

Suture cutter

Arthroscope

Cannula

Actions:

Secure repair construct

Deliver arthroscopic knot or knotless construct

Tension loop to secure tissue

Reinforce construct if tying knots

Back up repair with 3 or 4 half hitches

Advance and seat each half hitch sequentially

Cut suture tails

Introduce suture cutter

Cut suture tails completely

Completion_criteria:

Inferior capsulolabral tissue visibly reduced to the glenoid

Fixation visibly seated at the repair site

Suture tails cut

Errors:

Failure to create tissue indentation (SENTINEL)

Loose construct

Visible gaps between throws

Knot abuts cartilage (SENTINEL)

Suture breakage during cutting

Typical_duration:
1–2 minutes
