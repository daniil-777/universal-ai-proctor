Description of a Scene
Arthroscopic intra-articular shoulder scene during Bankart repair, viewed mainly from a posterior portal. Visible structures include the humeral head, glenoid, anterior-inferior labrum, capsule, and glenoid neck. Instruments enter through anterior portals for probing, tissue mobilization, glenoid preparation, anchor insertion, suture passage, fixation, and final review of the repaired labrum.
