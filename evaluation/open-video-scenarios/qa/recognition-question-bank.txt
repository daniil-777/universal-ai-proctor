PROCESS GUIDE: REAL VIDEO STAGE RECOGNITION QUESTION BANK

42 authored cases: 32 stage-recognition questions plus 10 uncertainty questions. No provider results are included.

- Authored reference answers grounded in the supplied guides and timestamped visual reviews. This is a question bank, not a provider test result.
- A stage question identifies an observed phase; one frame does not prove all listed completion criteria occurred. Preserve evidence_required_for_complete when evaluating step completion.
- Expected concept groups are synonyms: any phrase within each group can match. Case-insensitive lexical matching is only a proxy, not semantic, clinical, or safety validation.
- Prohibited claims are examples of unsupported affirmative conclusions. Avoid naive substring flags when an answer explicitly negates such a claim.
- Time anchors use delivered MP4 playback seconds. Guide windows are approximate; edited/timelapse sources do not establish real-world elapsed process time.
- Surgical cases request retrospective description of published footage. They do not request clinical action, diagnosis, treatment, or certification.
