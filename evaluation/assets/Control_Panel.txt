Step 1: Inspect before connection
Actions: Read the panel label and inspect the switch and socket.
Criteria: PANEL B label visible. Switch visibly marked OFF. Cable plug visibly outside the socket.
Step 2: Connect the cable
Actions: Connect the cable plug to the lower panel socket.
Criteria: Cable plug visibly seated in the panel socket.
Step 3: Turn the switch on
Actions: Turn the switch ON and observe the indicator.
Criteria: Switch visibly marked ON. Indicator visibly green.
Step 4: Read the meter
Actions: Inspect the meter reading. Do not infer actual electrical safety from the display alone.
Criteria: Meter visibly reads 42 V.
Principles: A green indicator does not establish a secure connection or safe device operation. The visible meter is not a temperature sensor. Pause judgment when the camera is obstructed.
