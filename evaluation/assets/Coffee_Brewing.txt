Coffee brewing — pour over
Principles
Use clean equipment.
Avoid contact with hot water.
Step 1 — Prepare the filter
Objective
Set up the brewer.
Actions
Place the filter inside the dripper.
Rinse the filter and discard the rinse water.
Completion Criteria
The filter is fitted and rinsed.
Step 2 — Add ground coffee
Actions
Weigh the ground coffee.
Add the coffee to the filter and level the bed.
Completion Criteria
Ground coffee is visible in the filter.
Step 3 — Bloom and pour
Actions
Wet the coffee bed evenly with hot water.
Continue pouring slowly in small circles.
Completion Criteria
The coffee bed is wet.
Water has been poured over the coffee.
Step 4 — Finish and serve
Actions
Allow the water to drain.
Remove the dripper and serve the coffee.
Completion Criteria
The dripper has been removed.
Coffee is present in the serving vessel.
